# Pythagorean Theorem
15

In a right triangle, a^2 + b^2 = c^2, where c is the hypotenuse opposite the right angle.
A 3-4-5 triangle is the classic whole-number example (9 + 16 = 25).
The converse also works: if the squares of two sides add up to the square of the third, the triangle is a right triangle.
