# Utilitarianism vs Kantian Ethics
21

Utilitarianism (Bentham, Mill) judges actions by their consequences and aims to maximize overall happiness.
Kant judges actions by duty: act only on rules you could will to be universal law (the categorical imperative).
The trolley problem shows the split: a utilitarian may divert the trolley, while a Kantian worries about using a person merely as a means.
